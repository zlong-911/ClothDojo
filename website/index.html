METHOD

Human supervision becomes a data generator.

Canonical surface cues guide manipulation. Corrections address visited failure states. Task metrics select trajectories for downstream training.

Canonical surface cues

NOCS colors encode canonical garment surface positions through the policy’s image interface, helping it identify corresponding parts under deformation.

Learn from visited states

Humans review failed rollouts and provide targeted corrections. These complement the initial demonstrations during supervised fine-tuning.

Retain and re-render

Task metrics screen successful autonomous trajectories. Their recorded states and actions support re-rendering with varied visual appearances.

See the NOCS, mirror augmentation, and HIL experiments ↓
